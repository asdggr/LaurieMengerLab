Discovery platform

Screen broadly.
Find the critical regulators.

Which genes shape the response of a T cell in a living system?

Our in vivo genome-wide CRISPR approach enables systematic investigation of the mechanisms that regulate primary T cells. It provides a foundation for finding targets that may be missed by candidate-led approaches.

We developed an optimized protocol to generate pools of Cas9-expressing murine T cells with genome-wide gene inactivation, using a retroviral guide-RNA library.

Cell engineering

Help donor T cells
withstand rejection.

How can allogeneic CAR T cells persist in the face of immune rejection?

Donor-derived CAR T cells could enable standardized batches of therapeutic cells. A key obstacle is their rejection by the recipient's immune system.

In preclinical work, genome-wide screening identified Fas and B2m as regulators of allogeneic T-cell survival. Further experiments investigated FAS editing as a way to protect CAR T cells from rejection without increasing susceptibility to natural killer cells.

T-cell biology

Understand the brakes
on CD4+ T cells.

What controls the expansion of antigen-experienced CD4+ T cells?

We use genome-wide CRISPR screens to investigate the regulatory mechanisms that shape CD4+ T-cell responses during localized immune responses.

This approach identified SOCS1 as an intrinsic checkpoint limiting CD4+ T-cell proliferation, providing a route to explore how immune regulation can inform cell-therapy engineering.
